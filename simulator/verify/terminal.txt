# ADBShellScreen terminal E2E: light-mode VT terminal over a real PTY.
# Needs a real phone plugged into the PC (the sim adb transport is libusb).
# Connect -> device screen -> Shell, then drive the on-screen keyboard by
# tapping keys (R2 q..p y=972, R3 a..l y=1059, R4 z..' y=1146, R5 y=1232;
# Enter=(674,1059), Ctrl=(46,1059), space=(340,1232)).
